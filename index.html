      Our Process

      Four steps. One accountable team.

      From the first conversation to the day we hand you the keys, one dedicated team stays with your project.

      01020304
      1. 01

        Consultation

        We start by listening — to your goals, your budget, and the land itself. A site visit and feasibility review set a clear direction.

        • Site visit
        • Feasibility study
        • Budget plan
        Duration 1–2 weeks
      2. 02

        Design

        Our architects and interior designers turn ideas into detailed plans, 3D visualisations, and material selections you can see and feel.

        • Architectural drawings
        • 3D renders
        • Material palette
        Duration 4–8 weeks
      3. 03

        Build

        Our own site team builds to the plan with weekly progress updates, strict quality checks, and a single point of contact.

        • Project management
        • Quality control
        • Weekly reports
        Duration 6–14 months
      4. 04

        Handover

        We finish every detail, walk you through your new home, and stay on call with an after-care warranty.

        • Final walkthrough
        • Documentation
        • Warranty support
